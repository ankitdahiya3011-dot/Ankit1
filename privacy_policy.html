Moonlit Roasters Privacy Policy

Moonlit Roasters respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our coffee shop, contact us, or use any services we provide.

Information we may collect We may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, and the contents of messages you send us. If you place an order or make a payment through a service we offer, relevant order and transaction details may also be processed. Payment card details are handled by the applicable payment provider; we do not intend to store full card details. If you visit a website or use an online service operated by us, technical information such as your IP address, device and browser details, and information about how you use the service may be collected, including through cookies where applicable.

How we use your information We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide products and services, process and manage orders and payments, maintain our records, improve our services, and meet legal obligations. We will send marketing messages only where permitted by law. You can opt out of marketing at any time by following the instructions in the message or contacting us.

Lawful basis Under UK data protection law, we process personal information where necessary to fulfil a contract, take steps at your request before entering into a contract, comply with a legal obligation, pursue our legitimate interests, or where you have given consent. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Sharing your information We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as payment, ordering, delivery, website, and IT providers. They may use information only as needed to provide services to us and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information when required by law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. We do not sell your personal information.

International transfers If a service provider processes your information outside the United Kingdom, we will take steps required by data protection law to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

How long we keep information We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why it was collected.

Cookies If our online services use cookies or similar technologies, they may be used to make the service work, remember preferences, or understand how it is used. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where required, we will ask for consent before using non-essential cookies.

Keeping information secure We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to access your personal information, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, request transfer of information, or withdraw consent. To exercise a right, contact Moonlit Roasters through the contact channel you used to reach us. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children Our services are not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will apply from the date it is made available.

Contact For questions or requests about this policy or your personal information, contact Moonlit Roasters using the contact details or communication channel through which you interact with us.
